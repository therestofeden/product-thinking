## 2026-07-05 — Discovery cluster (strategist, product-analyst, kohavi, prfaq-writer, prfaq-researcher)

Third release. Five agents that form the discovery layer — the chain between "I have a hunch" and "I have a defensible position." Strategist sets direction and writes a Mission Brief. Product-analyst gathers evidence. Kohavi (named after Ron Kohavi) challenges the causal logic, checks whether you're claiming to know something you only believe, and asks what the cheapest experiment would be to find out. The PRFAQ loop (prfaq-writer + prfaq-researcher) is the concrete artifact: writer drafts, researcher annotates with axiom violations and epistemic status labels (KNOWN / BELIEVED / ASSUMED), writer revises, default two rounds. The loop exists to catch ungrounded causal claims before they harden into a roadmap. [Post →](LINKEDIN_URL)

## 2026-06-28 — Caronte

Second release. HR director and capability auditor for the agent stack. Gatekeeps new agents and skills through a structured rubric before anything is installed. Runs a weekly curation pass every Monday proposing PROMOTE / MODIFY / RETIRE changes — never acting unilaterally. Includes the Mignolo Feed: reads 30 days of Mignolo daily brief digests to surface recurring work topics with no agent coverage. The system learns what it's missing by watching what you actually do.
